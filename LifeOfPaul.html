The Collection

Every card carries two signals. The picture shows what the moment felt like — calm, turbulent, or dark. The foil sheen and frame show whether God's will was advancing. They do not always agree, and that is the point. Press Show God's Hand to see only the gold.

The Timeline

Sixty-three years, one tick to the year. Each card hangs from the year its event began, and the bar beneath the axis shows how long it ran — so you can see what overlapped what. Whatever sits at the centre is magnified; the years at either edge stay small.
